Sign up and you can try every core XCONDA feature right away.

Also shipping officially — Art Director Pro

Cubeberry's in-house 9-cut auto storyboard is the first in the world to implement 'basic causality'. The result of one cut automatically becomes the reference for the next, so the story carries like a film.

The 'context collapse' problem of existing AI tools

Existing tools do not understand the link between cuts, so a character's clothes change or the situation resets. Art Director Pro keeps visual and situational information consistent across every cut.

  • The coffee stain from a spill in the café scene is still there in the next cut
  • A character soaked by heavy rain stays wet even in the following indoor scene

This is AI that understands the logic of a story rather than just generating images, and XCONDA is the only commercially available service that implements it.

Next step — ontology-based refinement

A next version is in development that refines the causality technology on an ontology basis. By structurally defining and reasoning about the relationships between objects, characters and situations in a scene, it will hold context far more precisely than the current version. Cubeberry plans to grow this into the core engine of its next product.

What the CEO said

CEO Jinseo Lee explained that causality-based technology solves "contextual continuity" — the problem existing AI tools could not. He added that the ontology work will take AI beyond generating scenes to the level of understanding a story.
