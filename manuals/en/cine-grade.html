— Purpose —

What does CineGrade AI do?

CineGrade AI applies the color grading, contrast, and mood of a reference movie still to your own photo. Upload the photo you want to edit and a style reference — the AI reads the color direction of the reference and transforms the original photo into that new tone.

— Why —

Why use this feature?

Because you can change only the color and atmosphere — quickly — while keeping the photo's composition and subject intact. Useful for testing a look before final color grading, or for showing clients several tone candidates with minimal turnaround.

— Composition Preserved —

Subject untouched, color shifted

Composition, subject, and pose stay as in the original — only color, contrast, and mood change. Test grading direction quickly without rebuilding the shot.

— Faster Color Work —

Pre-test looks before color grade

Simulate the look you want before going into the actual color grading session — fewer decision rounds, less time spent on the final pass.

When to use it

Use CineGrade AI when you want to match a cinematic look, an ad campaign tone, a lookbook mood, or a social-feed visual tone using a single reference. Ideal for unifying the color direction across multiple shots, or for testing footage in a completely different genre's mood.

— Layout —

Basic screen layout

The Your Photo / Reference Movie Still area shows two upload boxes side by side. The left is the photo to edit; the right is the style reference that defines the color and mood. The Generate button stays disabled until both images are ready.

— Left Box —

Upload the photo to edit

The box where you place the original photo the grade will be applied to. It supplies the composition and subject of the result.

— Right Box —

Upload the style reference

The box for the style reference (a movie still or any image whose tone you like). It defines the color, contrast, and mood of the result.

— Activation —
Both boxes must be filled before Generate activates.

Chapter 07Your Photo
P. 007
07
— Your Photo —

Upload the photo to edit

The left box is where you upload the original photo to be graded. Photos with a clear subject, clean background, and stable exposure make it easier to evaluate the reference grade on top.

— Upload —

Register the photo

Click the left box or drag and drop a file to upload the photo to grade. Once uploaded, a preview replaces the placeholder inside the box.

— Guideline —

What makes a good photo

Photos with a clear subject and background, and stable exposure, make the grade easier to evaluate. Shots that are heavily under- or overexposed will make tonal changes hard to read.

— Reset —

Remove the upload

The uploaded photo can be removed with the X button in the top-right of the box and replaced.

— Recommended —
Well-exposed portraits or landscapes with retained detail produce the most reliable grading results.

Chapter 08Reference Still
P. 008

— Reference —

Upload the style reference

The right box is for a movie still or any image with the color tone you want. The reference carries color, contrast, and lighting mood — not composition.

— Role —

Color · contrast · lighting signal

The reference passes color, contrast, and lighting mood to the result. It's a visual instruction saying “give me this tone”, not “give me this composition.”

— Preferred Format —

A frame with a clear tone

A single image with a clear tonal direction — warm, cold, cinematic, high-contrast — works best. Movie stills, ad key visuals, and art photos all work.

— Reset —

Remove the upload

The uploaded reference can be removed with the X button in the top-right of the box and replaced with another.

— Recommended —
When picking a reference, ask yourself whether you want the photo to take on the reference's color, lighting, and contrast — that's the filter.

Chapter 09Both Ready
P. 009

— Both Ready —

When both images are ready

Once both the original photo and the style reference are uploaded, Generate activates. In that state, the AI preserves the subject of the left photo while applying the color mood and contrast of the right image.

— Activation —

Generate turns on

When both boxes are filled, the Generate button turns yellow and becomes active. Grading can be triggered from this state.

— Processing Logic —

Left subject + right tone

The AI keeps subject, composition, and detail from the left photo, while using the right image's color mood and contrast as the input signal.

— Key —
Left = what. Right = in which tone.

Chapter 10Remove

— Remove —

Removing uploaded images

Press the X button in the top-right corner of each upload box to remove that image. Use this to swap a wrong photo or replace the reference.

— X Button —

Top-right of each box

A red X button appears in the top-right corner of each of the two upload boxes. Pressing it removes that image.

— Use —

Swap / replace

Use this to swap a wrong photo or replace the reference with a different tone. If only one side is removed, Generate deactivates again.

— Note —
Removing either image disables Generate again — re-upload to restore activation.

Chapter 11Generate

— Generate —

Generate

With both images uploaded, press Generate to start the color transfer. On the current test screen, each generation uses 6 credits.

— Condition —

Both images required

Both the Your Photo and Reference Movie Still boxes must be filled for Generate to activate.

— Cost —

Credit deduction

Each generation deducts 6 credits on the current test screen. Generation will not proceed if your credit balance is insufficient.

— Best Practice —
Review both images once more before pressing Generate — it cuts down re-runs.

Chapter 12Confirm
P. 012
12
— Confirm —

Confirmation dialog

Pressing Generate opens the “Would you like to generate an image?” confirmation. OK starts generation; Cancel returns to the previous screen.

— OK —

Start generation

OK kicks off the actual generation and deducts credits. Progress is shown in the result area while it runs.

— Cancel —

Return without generating

Cancel returns to the previous screen without generating. Both uploaded images remain intact, so you can edit and retry immediately.

— Safety —
The confirmation step exists to prevent accidental credit spend. Review, then press OK.

Chapter 13Result Card
P. 013

— Result Card —

Result card

When generation finishes, the transformed image appears as a card in the right-hand result area. The result card preserves the subject of the original photo while reflecting the color and atmosphere of the reference.

— Display —

New card on the right

A new card is added to the right-hand result area each time generation completes. It contains the freshly graded image.

— Stacking —

Tone candidates accumulate

Generate multiple times with different references to stack result cards — compare several tone candidates of the same photo in one view.

— Workflow —
Compare candidates → download only the final pick → delete the rest. That's the efficient close-out flow.

Chapter 14Review
P. 014

— Review —

Reviewing the output

The final image is the reference still's warm lighting and cinematic contrast applied to the original photo. Before final use, check that face tone, outfit color, background brightness, and overall contrast match your intent.

— Preserved —

Subject & composition held

The output keeps the photo's subject, composition, and detail. Only color shifts — the content of the photo stays consistent.

— Applied —

Reference tone & contrast

The reference's lighting mood, color tone, and contrast are overlaid on the result. Results may not be a 100% match — generate multiple takes and pick the best one.

— Inspect —

Face · outfit · background

Before final use, verify that face tone, outfit color, background brightness, and overall contrast match intent. If the image got too dark or oversaturated, regenerate with a different reference.

— Checkpoints —
Face tone · outfit color · background brightness · overall contrast — check these four first.

— Manage —

Download / Delete

Hovering over a result image reveals the download and delete icons. Download saves the result; delete removes the selected result.

— Download —

Download icon

Pressing the download icon saves the result image as a local file. Use this for any candidate you plan to take outside the app.

— Delete —

Delete icon

The delete icon removes the selected result card. Clearing rejected tone candidates keeps the result panel tidy.

— Reveal —
Icons appear only on hover — the result otherwise stays a clean image.

— Confirm Delete —

Delete confirmation

Pressing the delete icon opens the “Delete this media?” confirmation. Delete removes it; Cancel keeps the result.

— Delete —

Confirm deletion

Delete permanently removes the selected card. Deleted results are not recoverable, so confirm with care.

— Cancel —

Keep the result

Cancel leaves the card intact. It's safer to keep candidates until comparisons against other tones are complete.

— Wrap-Up —
Final pick downloaded → unneeded cards cleared → session done. That's the standard closing sequence.
