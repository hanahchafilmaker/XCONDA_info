— Purpose —

What does SpinAngle do?

SpinAngle generates the same scene from a different perspective using one image as the base. You mark camera position with a circle and viewing direction with an arrow — the AI reads those marks and produces a photorealistic image from that new camera angle.

SpinAngle Workflow
Mark the camera position and viewing direction on the source image, and the system generates the same scene from that new perspective.

— Why —

Why use this feature?

Test the same person and scene from different perspectives without reshooting or building a new 3D set. When you need to compare multiple angle candidates — storyboards, ad shots, lookbooks, video previz — production time drops significantly.

— No Reshoot —

Multiple angles from one image

Starting from a single source image, transform the same person and scene into multiple camera angles for comparison. Scale variants without recalling the model or location.

— No 3D Set —

Viewpoint change without sets

No physical set or 3D modeling required for a perspective change — pre-production concept validation cost drops significantly.

— Use Cases —

When to use it

Use SpinAngle when you want to shift a front-facing shot to a low or side angle, or move the camera around a person/product shot. Ideal for finding a more dynamic cut while keeping the scene's mood, or for showing clients multiple angle candidates at once.

Left side menu

Basic screen layout

On first entry, the Upload an image to get started area is displayed. Click the area or drag an image in to upload the source.

Upload an image to get started

Chapter 07Source Image

— Source —

Upload the source image

Once a source image is uploaded, it appears on the left canvas and the Circle, Arrow, Clear, and New Image buttons appear below it.

— Upload —

Register the image

Drag a file into the upload area or click to pick one. Once uploaded, the image appears on the canvas and is ready to be annotated with a camera position and direction.

— Tools Revealed —

Bottom tool buttons

After the image is displayed, the Circle, Arrow, Clear, and New Image buttons appear under the canvas so you can start marking position and direction.

— Guideline —

What makes a good source

Results are most stable when the subject is clearly visible. Heavy motion blur, severe noise, or busy backgrounds may reduce output quality.

— Recommended —
Images with clear subject outlines and a strong sense of space produce the most reliable results.

Chapter 08Status

— Status —

Circle = Camera / Arrow = Direction

The two status indicators at the top show whether the camera position and direction have both been set. They start empty, and turn into green checks once you draw the circle and arrow.

— Circle Status —

Circle = Camera

When a circle is drawn on the canvas, the Circle = Camera indicator switches to a green check, meaning the camera position is registered.

— Arrow Status —

Arrow = Direction

When an arrow is drawn, the Arrow = Direction indicator switches to a green check, meaning the viewing direction is registered.

— Activation —
Both indicators must turn into green checks before Generate activates.

Chapter 09Circle Tool

— Circle Tool —

Circle tool

Select Circle, then draw a circle on the image to mark where the camera will sit. The circle is the anchor point of the new perspective.

— Select —

Activate Circle mode

Click the Circle button in the bottom toolbar to enter Circle mode. Once active, you can draw a circle on the canvas.

— Mark —

Draw the camera position

Drag on the image to draw a circle. This circle defines the anchor point of the new viewpoint — place it thinking about which side of the subject you want the shot to come from.

— Placement Tip —

Think subject-relative

Where you place the circle — left, right, above, below the subject — changes the resulting angle. Imagine “the camera sits here” when placing it for the most intentional output.

— Meaning —
Circle position = the virtual camera's location. It defines direction and distance from the subject.

Chapter 10Arrow Tool
P. 010
10
— Arrow Tool —

Arrow tool

Select Arrow, then draw an arrow on the image to indicate which direction the camera looks toward. A new perspective can only be computed when both the circle and the arrow are present, so draw both before pressing Generate.

— Select —

Activate Arrow mode

Click the Arrow button in the bottom toolbar to enter Arrow mode. Once active, you can draw an arrow on the canvas.

— Mark —

Draw the viewing direction

Draw an arrow from the circle toward the subject. The arrow represents the camera's line of sight and shapes the composition of the result.

— Paired Input —

Circle + Arrow = new perspective

Both circle and arrow must be present for the AI to compute a new perspective. Draw both, then press Generate. With only one mark, Generate stays disabled.

— Meaning —
Arrow = where the camera is looking. If the circle is the “where,” the arrow is the “toward what.”

Chapter 11Tools
P. 011
11
— Tools —

Tool buttons

Circle marks the camera position, Arrow marks the viewing direction, Clear resets the marks, and New Image replaces the current source image.

— Circle —

Mark camera position

A tool that places the virtual camera by drawing a circle.

— Arrow —

Mark viewing direction

A tool that defines where the camera looks by drawing an arrow.

— Clear —

Reset the marks

Removes both the circle and arrow from the canvas. Use this to redo the position and direction. The source image stays in place.

— New Image —

Replace the source

Pressing New Image replaces the current source image with another so you can start over with a different image. Any previously drawn circle and arrow are reset.

Chapter 12Generate
P. 012
12
— Generate —

Generate

Generate activates once both the camera position and direction are set. On the current test screen, each generation uses 6 credits.

— Condition —

Both circle and arrow required

Circle = Camera and Arrow = Direction must both show green checks for Generate to activate.

— Cost —

Credit deduction

Each generation deducts 6 credits on the current test screen. Generation will not proceed if your credit balance is insufficient.

— Best Practice —
Review the circle and arrow positions once more before pressing Generate — it cuts down re-runs.

Chapter 13Confirm
P. 013
13
— Confirm —

Confirmation dialog

Pressing Generate opens the “Would you like to generate an image?” confirmation. OK starts generation; Cancel returns to the previous screen.

— OK —

Start generation

OK kicks off the actual generation and deducts credits. Progress is shown in the result area while it runs.

— Cancel —

Return without generating

Cancel returns to the previous screen without generating. The circle and arrow you drew remain intact, so you can edit and retry immediately.

— Safety —
The confirmation step exists to prevent accidental credit spend. Review, then press OK.

— Result Card —

Result card

When generation finishes, the new-perspective image appears as a card in the right-hand result area. Repeat generations stack in the right area so you can compare angle candidates.

— Display —

New card on the right

A new card is added to the right-hand result area each time generation completes. Download and delete icons appear at the bottom-right of the card.

— Stacking —

Angle candidates accumulate

Multiple generations stack so you can compare different angle candidates of the same scene in one view.

— Workflow —
Compare candidates → download only the final pick → delete the rest. That's the efficient close-out flow.

— Review —

Reviewing the output

The result preserves the original mood while reflecting the camera position and direction you marked, producing the scene from a new angle. Before final use, check that face, outfit, background distortion, and the subject's orientation match your intent.

— Preserved —

Original mood held

The result keeps the source's overall mood, tone, and color. Only the perspective shifts — the scene's atmosphere stays consistent.

— Applied —

New camera angle reflected

The circle's position and arrow's direction are reflected as the new camera angle. Results may not be a 100% match — generate multiple takes and pick the best one.

— Inspect —

Distortion & orientation

Before final use, verify that face, outfit, and background distortion, along with the subject's gaze and posture, match your intent. If something looks off, adjust the circle/arrow positions and regenerate.

— Checkpoints —
Face proportions · outfit details · background perspective · subject orientation — check these four first.

— Manage —

Download / Delete

Download and delete icons appear at the bottom-right of each result card. Download saves the generated result; delete removes the selected result from the list.

— Download —

Download icon

Pressing the download icon saves the result image as a local file. Use this for any candidate you plan to take outside the app.

— Delete —

Delete icon

The delete icon removes the selected result from the list. Clearing rejected takes keeps the result panel tidy.

— Position —
Both icons sit at the bottom-right of each result card.

— Confirm Delete —

Delete confirmation

Pressing the delete icon opens the “Are you sure you want to delete all selected media?” confirmation. OK removes it; Cancel keeps the result.

— OK —

Confirm deletion

OK permanently removes the selected card. Deleted results are not recoverable, so confirm with care.

— Cancel —

Keep the result

Cancel leaves the card intact. It's safer to keep candidates until comparisons against other angles are complete.

— Wrap-Up —
Final pick downloaded → unneeded cards cleared → session done. That's the standard closing sequence.
