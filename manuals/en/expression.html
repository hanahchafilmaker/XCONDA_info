— Camera Options —

Setting view, shot size, and angle

Combine three camera conditions — Camera View, Shot Size, and Camera Angle — to render the same expression in different framings. All are optional.

— 1. Camera View / Position —

Facing direction

Choose which way the subject faces the camera — Front View, 3/4 View, Profile View, etc. Use Front View for a frontal cut and 3/4 View for a slightly turned cut.

— 2. Shot Size / Distance —

Size in frame

Choose how large the subject appears — Wide Shot, Medium Shot, Close-Up, etc. Use the Close-Up range to emphasize expression, and the Medium range to show outfit and pose.

— 3. Camera Angle / Height —

Camera height & gaze

Choose the camera height and feel of the gaze — Eye Level, Low Angle, High Angle, etc. Eye Level feels natural, while Low Angle makes the subject look stronger.

— Optional —
All camera options are optional. If you only need an expression change, you can leave them empty and proceed.

— Generate —

Running generation and checking progress

Once the original and prompt are ready, press Generate (6 credits), then check the state via the confirmation popup and progress rate.

— Generate —

Generate button activates

Once the original image and prompt are ready, the Generate button activates. Each generation uses 6 credits.

— Confirm —

Confirmation popup

The popup Would you like to generate an image? appears. Press OK to start, or Cancel to return to the previous screen.

— Progress —

Generation progress

Creating your image… shows the progress as a percentage. On completion, an image card is added to the right result area.

— Credits —
6 credits per generation. Consider credit usage when generating several expression candidates.

Chapter 10Download & Tips

— Download & Tips —

Saving results and stable usage

Download or tidy results with the bottom icons, and protect identity by testing the expression first, then adding camera options when needed.

— Download / Delete —

Download and tidy up

Use the download icon at the bottom of the result area to download images, and the trash icon to tidy up results.

— Tip —
Changing expression, gaze, framing, and lighting all at once can weaken the original identity. It is more stable to test the expression only first, then add camera options when needed.
