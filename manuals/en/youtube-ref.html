— Purpose —

Purpose of YouTube Ref

When a client sends a reference video saying "make it like this," YouTube Ref quickly visualizes the tone and mood to produce a storyboard draft.

— Traditional method —
Watch the video repeatedly, manually capture keyframes and organize
→ Time-consuming, reliant on subjective interpretation
— YouTube Ref method —
AI auto-generates a 9-scene storyboard from a link alone
→ Quick draft, useful for sharing direction within the team

— Use Cases —

When to use YouTube Ref

Reference analysis, planning meeting drafts, storyboard production, and benchmark analysis.

01

Client reference analysis

Quickly identify the visual tone, composition, and color of a reference video sent by a client.

02

Internal planning draft

Set direction based on a reference video during the planning phase and share with team members.

03

Storyboard draft

Visually confirm the full flow before shooting or production.

04

Competitor / benchmark

Analyze competitor ad videos to identify structure and direction patterns.

— Note —
YouTube Ref does not accurately reflect the actual content context of the original video. Use it as a visual tone-and-mood reference only.

— Key Features —

Key features of YouTube Ref

Link input → 9-scene auto-generation, tone-and-mood extraction, auto scene splitting, character reference, per-scene editing.

— 01 —

Link → 9-scene auto-generation

Enter a YouTube video link and AI analyzes the video to generate a 9-keyframe storyboard.

— 02 —

Tone-and-mood extraction

Images are generated based on the visual atmosphere — color, lighting, and composition — of the video.

— 03 —

Auto scene splitting

AI automatically divides the video flow into 9 scenes to compose the sequence.

— 04 —

Character reference (optional)

Upload Body/Outfit and Face Detail images to have the character reflected in the storyboard.

Chapter 05Prompt Tips

— Prompt Tips —

Prompt input guidelines

When editing scene descriptions (Regenerate), including specific space/environment information improves accuracy.

— Good examples —
"Students attending class inside a classroom"
"A rainy night in a downtown street, neon sign lighting"
"A person running on a beach with the sunset behind"
— Avoid —
"Indoors" → Not specific about what kind of interior
"Outside" → Environment is vague
"Nice atmosphere" → Abstract expression AI cannot interpret
— Key Principle —
Describe place + situation + mood concretely. Use visually evocative words rather than abstract expressions.
