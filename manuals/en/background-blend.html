— Purpose —

Purpose of Cinematic Image Blender

Used to generate cinematic still images by AI-blending character photos and background images.

— Traditional method —

Manual compositing in Photoshop

Manual compositing in Photoshop or similar tools requires time-consuming work to match lighting and color tones.

— Cinematic Image Blender method —

Person + Background → Auto-blended by AI

Just upload a person and a background image — AI auto-matches lighting, color, and shadows. The character's face, skin tone, hairstyle, and clothing are preserved while blended into the background.

— Features —

Feature overview

Person + background auto-blending, person preservation, cinematic tone application.

— 01 —

Person + background auto-blending

When you upload a person image and a background image, AI automatically matches lighting, color, and shadows for blending.

Cinematic tone applied

Cinematic color grading and lighting are automatically applied to the blended result.

Chapter 05Prompt Tips

— Prompt Tips —

Prompt input guidelines

Describe the character's action, lighting mood, and cinematic detail concretely.

— Good examples —
"Standing by the window, rim lighting, emotional expression"
"Walking through the hallway, dramatic overhead light, cinematic mood"
— Avoid —
"Blend it" → Instruction AI cannot interpret
"Make it pretty" → Not visually specific
— Key Principle —
Describe the character's pose / action + lighting direction / mood + camera feel concretely. Person images with no background or solid-color backgrounds yield higher blending accuracy.
