The 3 focus modes — Dialogue (character-driven), Action (movement-driven), and Atmosphere (space / mood-driven) — determine the directional approach of the result. Detailed descriptions of each mode follow on the next pages.

DIALOGUE

ACTION

— Mode 03 —

ATMOSPHERE
FOCUS

— Scenario —

Write the scenario

Enter the situation and flow of the content as text. The role name of each character must be included.

— Step 01 —

Click and type

Click the Scenario field once and enter the situation and flow of the content as text.

— Step 02 —

Include role names

When writing the scenario, the character's role name (Role name) must be included. The role name ensures faces and wardrobes are applied correctly.

— Required —
The Scenario is mandatory. The input must exceed a minimum character count, and the Generate button will not activate if it is empty.

— Face ID Source —

Upload character photo

This step generates 9 cinematic cuts with visual continuity through facial recognition (face lock).

— Step 01 —

Upload photo

Click or drag the gray field inside the yellow frame to upload the character's photo.

— Recommended —

Use front-facing photos

Use a front-facing photo where possible to ensure accurate face lock.

— Note —
Animation characters can also be used, but since the Nano Banana-based engine is optimized for live-action data, style transformation (live-action conversion) or detail loss may occur.

— Tab Delete · Tab Management —

Delete tabs and auto-fill empty slots

Click X in the red frame to delete a tab. When generating, empty slots are automatically filled even without selecting a tab.

— Step 01 —

Delete tab with X button

Click X in the red frame to delete the tab.

— Behavior —

Auto-fill empty slots

When generating without selecting a tab, empty slots are automatically filled. Example: delete tabs 1 and 3 → generate → images are auto-generated in the empty tabs.

— Targeted —

Generate to specific tab only

To delete a specific tab and generate only in its place — for example, after deleting tabs 1 and 3, to generate only in tab 3 — click tab 3 first, then click Generate.
