— Purpose —

Auto-generate angles from a single image

Real photo shoots require dozens of takes — changing lighting, camera position, and tripod height. Auto Angle Generator replaces this process with a single source image.

— Traditional method —

Lighting · camera · crew

Real shoots require dozens of takes — adjusting lighting, camera position, and tripod height across each variation.

— Auto Angle Generator —

Auto-convert from 1 source

Get product or character images from various angles without a location, equipment, or crew.

— Source —
1 Image
A single source image
— Output —
34 Angles
Up to 34 camera angles

Chapter 05Source Tips
P. 005
05
— Source Image Tips —

Source image preparation

For higher result quality, the source image should meet these conditions.

01

Simple background

White / solid backgrounds or clean studio backgrounds yield the highest angle conversion accuracy.

02

Centered subject

The subject should be centered. Cropped or off-center images may distort during conversion.

03

High resolution

Higher resolution yields better quality. Low-resolution sources lose detail in the result.

04

Frontal capture

Front-facing or slightly angled images are recommended. Extreme angles may convert unnaturally.

05

Single subject

Multiple overlapping subjects or complex hand / arm crossings reduce conversion quality.
