— Generation Mode —

Select Models and a generation mode

At the top of the Video Generator workspace screen, in the Models dropdown, check that Seedance 2.5is selected. Below it, click the chevron next to Mode to open the Generation mode list, then choose one of the four modes to suit your purpose.

Generation modeKey featuresRecommended use
Ref-to-videoCreate a new video from references (image, video, audio) and a prompt. Enter only a prompt with no references to generate a video from text aloneWhen creating a new scene from scratch (default mode)
Video editLoad an existing video and keep the elements you want, changing only the parts you wantWhen you want to change only some elements of a generated video, such as the weather, background, or mood
Video extendGenerate the next segment that continues naturally from the last scene of an existing video (repeatable extension)When you need a scene longer than 30 seconds or seamless continuous motion (a chase, dance, or camera move)
First/last frameSpecify a start image and an end image, and generate the movement between the two scenesWhen the first and last look of a scene are clearly decided

Text only (Text-to-Video)

There is no separate Text to video mode. In the default mode, Ref-to-video , entering only a prompt with no references generates a video from text alone. When generating from text only, check that the active reference send count (Sending: Reference N) is 0.

Video extend tips

If the source video you want to extend is long, trimming in only the last 8–10 secondsis efficient for managing reference size. In the prompt, do not describe the opening scene again — write only the action that happens next and the camera movement. If repeated extensions start to drift the people or background, extract the last scene that still looked natural as an image and continue in First/last frame mode for a more stable result.

— Active Reference —

Add references and assign @Image tags

+ Add: press it to upload an image, and a yellow check markappears at the top right of the thumbnail, and a unique call name such as @Image1is automatically assigned below it. Only checked files are actually sent to the model at generation time.

Use directly from the Reference tab

  1. In the media area you want (IMAGE / VIDEO / AUDIO), click + Addto upload a file.
  2. Check that the files you want to use for this generation have the yellow check turned on.
  3. Tag names are assigned in the order checked, such as @Image1, @Image2, @Video1 .

Load from the Asset Library

  1. Register frequently used materials in advance from the profile icon at the top right of the screen → Library → Uploads tab → + Add asset.
  2. In the Video Generator's Asset Library tab, click the materials you want to use to select them (N selected).
  3. At the bottom, click Use selected assetsto add them immediately as references for the current task.
— Tip —
Adding references is not a matter of adding as many as possible — it is better to select only the key materials. Adding too many at once dilutes the influence of each reference, so one face image per personis the most stable choice.

Chapter 06Write a prompt and call @
P. 006
06
— Prompt & @Tag —

Write a prompt and call @ tags

Prompt input field, write the scene for the video you want to generate. You can enter up to 30,000 characters, and the live character count (0 / 30000) is shown at the bottom right. Below the input field, a summary chip of the currently applied options (Draft on | 1:1 | 480p | 25s | High Quality on) is shown, and clicking the settings icon on the right opens the Options panel.

Specify references with @ input

While typing a prompt, type @and a list of the materials registered in Input Assets appears; clicking one inserts a tag such as @Image1, @Video1 into the sentence. Uploading a reference alone is not enough — if you do not call it in the prompt, the model cannot tell where to apply it, so always attach an @tag to the sentence where a person or background first appears to assign its role.

How to write elements to exclude (Negative Prompt alternative)

Seedance 2.5 has no separate Negative Prompt input field. If there is an element you do not want, instead of a negative such as "no ~", describe it as the positive state that should appear on screen.

· Example: "No camera shake" → "The camera is fixed in its original position (Locked-off shot)"

— Note —
If the prompt input field is empty, the Generate button below is greyed out and cannot be clicked.

Chapter 08Draft mode and prompt examples
P. 008
08
— Draft Mode & Examples —

Draft mode settings and practical prompt examples

Click the button at the top of the Options tab to switch it to the yellow Draft on state, and the Resolutionbelow is automatically fixed to a single 480p button. First test several 480p drafts at low cost and high speed, then pick the result you like and convert it to a 1080p final versionfrom the result card.

01. Text only (Ref-to-video, no references)
A woman walks alone down a rain-soaked alley at night, neon signs reflecting on the wet pavement. She stops in front of a glass door, hesitates, then pushes it open — warm golden light spills out into the alley. The camera follows her from behind at shoulder height.
Meaning: At night, a woman walks alone down a rain-soaked alley. Neon signs reflect on the wet pavement. She stops in front of a glass door, hesitates briefly, then pushes it open — warm golden light spills out into the alley. The camera follows her from behind at shoulder height.
02. Combining references (Ref-to-video)
@Image1 sits in the back seat of a taxi at night, city lights sliding across the window. She turns toward @Image2 and speaks quietly. Locked-off medium shot from the front seat, cool streetlight from the left.
Meaning: At night, @Image1 sits in the back seat of a taxi as city lights slide past the window. She turns toward @Image2 and speaks quietly. A locked-off medium shot from the front seat, with cool streetlight coming from the left.
03. Editing part of an existing video (Video edit — state what to keep and what to change)
Keep @Video1 exactly as it is — same people, same movement, same camera. Change only the weather: light snow falls throughout the shot.
Meaning: Keep @Video1 exactly as it is — the same people, same movement, same camera. Change only the weather: light snow falls throughout the shot.
04. Extending a video (Video extend) & 05. Connecting first/last frames (First/last frame)
[Video extend] Continue @Video1 from its last frame. The woman steps through the glass door into a warm, crowded bar and looks around the room. The camera keeps following her from behind at shoulder height, with the same lighting and mood.

[First/last frame] The woman walks slowly from the alley entrance toward the glass door as the rain keeps falling. Neon reflections ripple across the wet pavement with each step. Smooth, continuous camera movement following her, realistic night lighting.
Meaning: (Extension) Continuing from the last scene of @Video1, the woman steps through the glass door into a warm, crowded bar, looks around, and the camera follows her from behind in the same lighting and mood. / (First/last frame) Connects from the starting image at the alley entrance to the ending image in front of the glass door, with neon reflections on the rainy night street and a smooth camera follow.
— Caution —
Resolution cannot be changed in the prompt text. With Draft onenabled, even writing "Make it in 1080p" in the prompt still produces a 480p draft, so request the 1080p final version from the result card after generation is complete.

— Generate —

Run Generate and check credits

Once you have finished entering the prompt (for example: 7526 / 30000), the Generate button below turns yellow and becomes active. Check the summary chip at the top left (Draft on | 1:1 | 480p | 25s | High Quality on) and the credits to be consumed shown to the right of the button (for example: 725 credits), then click the yellow Generate button.

— Note —
The credits consumed are not a fixed value; they are calculated automatically based on the generation mode, video length (seconds), resolution, Draft setting, and High Quality setting, and shown in real time next to the button.

Chapter 11Generation queue and background execution

— Queue & Background —

Generation queue and background execution

Once the generation request is accepted, the result area shows a status message: "Your video is starting soon / Starting shortly" .

Notice: "You can leave this page. Your accepted request will keep running on the server." (Even if you leave this page, your accepted request will keep running on the server.) At the bottom, Continue in background is shown, along with a notice that generation has started and can no longer be cancelled ("Generation has started — cancel is no longer available.").

— Tip —
While the video is rendering, you do not need to keep the current window open. You can move to another menu or work on a new prompt in parallel, and the video will still complete safely in the server background.

— Progress & Results —

Check generation progress and manage results

Once rendering is fully under way, a badge showing the current generation spec (for example, the yellow DRAFT · 480p) appears at the top left of the result card, and below it, the text "Generating video" appears with the live progress percentage (for example: ~12%) and a yellow progress bar. Once generation reaches 100%, you can play or download the video from the result list on the right, or request a 1080p final version.

Feature after completionHow it works
Check meta informationIn the information panel next to each result, check the creation date (Date), resolution (Resolution), aspect ratio (Aspect Ratio), duration (Duration), and model used (Model).
Preview & full screenClick the Play button to play a preview inside the card, and click the video screen to expand it to full screen.
Request a 1080p final version (Draft)DRAFT · 480p— if you like a draft generated in this way, request a 1080p final-version conversionfrom that result card to complete the high-quality final video.
Download · Share · DeleteHover over the video to save it to your PC with the download (Save) icon, share it externally with the share icon, or delete unneeded cuts with the trash icon to tidy up your workspace.
— Note —
Generated results keep accumulating in the list on the right, and you can also view and manage them together in the Library > Generations tab under the profile menu at the top.
